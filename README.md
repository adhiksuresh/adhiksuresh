## Hi, I'm Adhik Suresh

I work on **AI and data-driven methods for smart grids and energy systems**: how learning, optimisation and real measurement data can make energy decisions more efficient and more trustworthy.

- **Education:** M.Tech in Energy Science and Technology, **IIT Hyderabad** (Smart Grid Lab, advisor: Dr. P. K. Yemula), 2024 · B.Tech in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University, 2022
- **Experience:** **Deputy Manager – Systems Engineering**, Adani Enterprises (Defence business unit), deputed to DRDO Research Centre Imarat, Hyderabad (Jul 2024 – Feb 2026): systems integration, hardware-in-the-loop validation and qualification testing for a national aerospace programme
- Currently **applying for PhD positions (2027)**

### Research interests
- Machine learning for energy: load forecasting, generative models for synthetic energy data, learning-based control under uncertainty
- EV charging infrastructure and EV–grid integration
- Demand response, time-of-day tariffs and the economics of the energy transition
- Design and optimisation of integrated multi-energy systems (electricity, heat, hydrogen, storage)

### Projects
| Project | What it does | Tools |
|---|---|---|
| [EV charging station analytics](https://github.com/adhiksuresh/ev-charging-station-analytics) | M.Tech thesis code: 15-minute 5G smart-meter data from four campus EV chargers, load profiles and time-of-day tariff costs (reproduces the published results), plus a 2026 extension testing anomaly detectors against synthetic meter-data attacks. | Python, pandas, scikit-learn |
| [Multi-energy system optimisation](https://github.com/adhiksuresh/multi-energy-system-optimisation) | Hourly, full-year model of a coupled electricity–hydrogen–heat system: what it costs to move from gas to net zero, with the original study and a corrected version. | PyPSA, HiGHS |
| [VAE for synthetic load profiles](https://github.com/adhiksuresh/vae-synthetic-load-profiles) | Variational autoencoder written from scratch (hand-derived gradients) that generates realistic daily load profiles, benchmarked against a Gaussian baseline. | NumPy |
| [GNN power-flow estimation](https://github.com/adhiksuresh/gnn-power-flow-estimation) | Graph convolutional network vs neural-network and DC power-flow baselines on the IEEE 30-bus system, including unseen line outages. An honest negative result. | PyTorch, pandapower |
| [Safe learning-augmented control](https://github.com/adhiksuresh/safe-learning-inverter-control) | What a safety filter actually does when online learning is added to frequency and voltage control, for one controller and for two sharing a grid. | Python |
